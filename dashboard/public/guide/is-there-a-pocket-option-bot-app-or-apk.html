Why there is no APK — and why to avoid one

Avalisa has to run inside the Pocket Option trading page, in your own browser, on your own account. A separate app cannot do that, so we have never published an APK or an app-store app.

If a website offers a “Pocket Option bot APK” or an “Avalisa app” as a file to download, it is not ours. A file from an unknown source can do anything on your phone and you cannot see what. Do not install it.

The only official places are the Chrome Web Store listing for desktop and avalisabot.vercel.app/webapp for phones.

How Avalisa runs on a phone

On a phone the bot comes in through a browser bookmark: you open Pocket Option in your phone's browser, tap the Avalisa bookmark, and the Avalisa panel opens at the bottom of the trading page. Open /webapp on the phone itself and it shows the exact steps for that phone. In outline:

  1. Open avalisabot.vercel.app/webapp in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and tap Copy bookmark code.
  2. Save a bookmark named Avalisa and paste the code in as its address.
  3. Use the Open Pocket Option button, sign in to Pocket Option and stay on the Demo account for your first run.
  4. On the trading page, run the Avalisa bookmark (iPhone: Bookmarks, then Avalisa; Android Chrome: type Avalisa in the address bar and tap the bookmark in the suggestions). Sign in to the panel with your Avalisa account and press Start.

If Pocket Option reloads, run the bookmark again; the bot picks up where it was. You need a free Avalisa account — create one here.

How it runs on a computer

On desktop, Avalisa PO Bot is a Google Chrome extension. Install it from the Chrome Web Store, sign in to the extension with your Avalisa account, open Pocket Option, and the Avalisa panel appears on the trading page. The setup guide walks through it.

Keep the trading page open

The bot has to see each trade's result to act on it. On a computer, keep the Pocket Option tab visible: a background or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection. On a phone the same applies — keep Pocket Option on screen while the bot trades, and press Stop when you are done.

Risk statement

Trading binary options is high risk. The bot does not guarantee profit and does not change the odds — at a 92% payout you need to win more than 52.1% of trades just to break even (1 / (1 + payout)). Martingale multiplies the stake after each loss and a losing streak can wipe out a balance: in the panel, Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle, so at 2x, Steps 3 costs 15 times the base stake. Capping Steps is the real limit; there is no separate stop-loss setting. Start on demo and only trade money you can afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an Avalisa app in the App Store or Google Play?

No. On a phone you use the free bookmark from /webapp; on a computer you use the Chrome extension.

Do I have to install anything on my phone?

No. You save one bookmark in Safari or Chrome. Nothing is downloaded.

Does Pocket Option allow bots?

Pocket Option's own blog says automated strategies are fine as long as they comply with platform rules, and names market manipulation — not automation — as the explicit ban risk. That is their blog, not their formal Terms of Service, which we have not read directly; for a definitive answer on your own account, ask Pocket Option support.

Can I use the same plan on phone and computer?

Yes — you sign in with the same Avalisa account on the phone panel and in the extension, and your plan is attached to that account.
