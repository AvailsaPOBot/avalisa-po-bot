The rule to read first

The bonus is not cash you can withdraw straight away. Pocket Option's bonus rules say:

  • Turnover comes first. To make a deposit bonus available for withdrawal, your net turnover must reach a set threshold. Hedged and copied trades do not count.
  • A withdrawal request cancels the bonus. If you ask to withdraw while a deposit bonus is active, the bonus is cancelled automatically and cannot be recovered.
  • Your own deposit stays yours. If the bonus is not fully worked off, the bonus funds and any bonus profit are removed, and you can still withdraw your original deposit and any profits made.
