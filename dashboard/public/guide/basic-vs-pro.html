What the two plans actually include

Demo (free). Ten Martingale trades, webapp bot access, dashboard access, all supported timeframes, and basic trade history. This is the account type we always recommend you start on.

Basic — $69, one-time only. Unlimited Martingale trades, cloud settings sync, and trade history. It never renews and you are never billed again. If Martingale is the only strategy you want, this is the whole product.

Pro — buyer chooses at checkout. Either $119 one-time (same never-renews structure as Basic) or $29/month (a recurring subscription that auto-renews until cancelled). Both Pro options unlock everything in Basic plus the Avalisa Bot mode: a rule-based signal mode with Low, Mid (3 of 4 rules), or High (all 4 rules) intensity settings.

So the question is simple: do you want to trade Martingale only (Basic), or do you also want the Avalisa Bot rule-based mode (Pro)?

Which plan fits your style

Basic fits you if:

  • You want unlimited Martingale automation and nothing else.
  • You want to pay once and never think about billing again.
  • You are comfortable choosing and adjusting your own Martingale parameters: direction (Always Buy, Always Sell, or Alternating), multiplier, step limit, timeframe, and minimum payout.

Pro fits you if:

  • You want a second strategy: the Avalisa Bot rule-based mode, which enters trades only when its configured rules (Low, Mid, or High intensity) line up.
  • You want more than one approach available in the same extension, without managing two tools.

Before you pay for anything: the risk you should know first

This section is more important than the comparison above. Read it.

  • Binary options are high risk. Most of your attention on this page should go to that sentence. Only trade money you can afford to lose.
  • The bot does not guarantee profit. No setting combination has been measured to beat break-even over large samples.
  • Martingale grows your stake after every loss. At a 3x multiplier, a 5-trade losing cycle costs 121× your base stake; at 2x, the same 5-trade cycle costs 31×. A streak of losses can wipe out a balance quickly. Limiting your Martingale Steps is the real way to cap a losing streak — there is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss-limit setting. When the next stake would exceed your balance, the bot pauses.
  • Demo-first is not politeness; it is procedure. The extension behaves the same way on demo and real accounts. Run your chosen settings on a demo account first, watch what the bot actually does, and only then consider real money. Before that, walk through a go/no-go checklist and stop at any step you cannot clear.
  • The Pocket Option tab must stay visible while the bot trades; a backgrounded or minimised tab can delay detection of trade results.
  • Pocket Option's own blog says users can use automated strategies as long as they comply with platform rules; that content does not specifically address third-party browser extensions and is not their formal Terms of Service. Neither side names third-party bots explicitly.

Practical steps

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Start on a Pocket Option demo account. Configure your strategy and run it long enough to see real behaviour, not one lucky streak.
  3. If you are using Martingale, set your Steps limit deliberately. Treat it as your risk cap. Understand the math: every step allows a much larger total cycle cost.
  4. Prefer higher payouts when choosing settings (the extension lets you set a minimum payout %).
  5. Only consider a paid plan after demo behaviour matches your expectations. Buy Basic for Martingale-only, or Pro if you specifically want the Avalisa Bot rule-based mode.
  6. Keep the tab visible while it trades.
  7. If anything goes wrong or something does not work as described, email avalisapobot@gmail.com with what you did and what you saw.

Frequently asked questions

Is Basic a subscription?

No. Basic is $69 one-time. It never renews and you are never billed again. Pro's $29/month option is the one recurring option and it auto-renews until cancelled; Pro's $119 option is one-time like Basic. See pricing questions answered.

Can I upgrade from Basic to Pro later?

The plans differ by one thing: the Avalisa Bot rule-based mode is Pro-only. Everything in Basic works in Pro. If you later want the rule-based mode, you are paying for that added capability.

Can I trust either plan with real money?

No. The bot does not guarantee profit. Binary options trading is high risk and Martingale amplifies losing streaks. Start on a demo account and size your stakes to money you can afford to lose.

What happens if a losing streak exceeds my balance?

The bot pauses when the next stake would exceed your balance. That is why the Steps limit matters: it bounds how deep a cycle can go before that happens.
