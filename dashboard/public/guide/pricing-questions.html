Pricing questions, answered

What is the difference between Basic and Pro?

Basic ($69) unlocks unlimited Martingale trading, cloud settings sync and trade history. Pro adds Avalisa Bot mode, the rule-based signal mode, alongside Martingale. Choose Basic if Martingale is all you need, Pro if you want both modes.

Is it a subscription?

Basic is a one-time $69 payment and never renews. For Pro you choose at checkout: $119 one-time, which also never renews, or $29/month, which is a recurring subscription that renews monthly until you cancel. Both Pro options unlock the same features.

What can I test with the free Demo plan?

Demo includes 10 Martingale trades, webapp bot access, the dashboard, all supported timeframes and basic trade history. Run it on a Pocket Option demo account first to see how the bot behaves before you pay.

How do I get Pro through Pocket Option?

Register a new Pocket Option account through our link (we earn a commission from Pocket Option when you do), then link that Pocket Option ID in your Avalisa dashboard under Settings. If Pocket Option has already confirmed the registration came through our link, Pro is granted straight away; otherwise the request waits for a manual review. Pro granted this way does not expire.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. On a computer Avalisa is a Chrome extension; on an iPhone or Android phone it runs through a free bookmark you save from avalisabot.vercel.app/webapp, which opens the Avalisa panel on the Pocket Option trading page.

Does it work on real accounts or only demo?

Both. The extension places trades the same way on a demo or a real Pocket Option account. Start on demo, and remember Martingale raises the stake after each loss, so a losing streak can empty a balance.

How do I cancel the $29/month Pro?

Cancel the subscription in Whop, where you bought it. When it ends, your licence returns to the free Demo plan; your account, settings and trade history are kept, and subscribing again upgrades it again. Basic and the $119 Pro option never renew, so there is nothing to cancel.

Does Avalisa need my Pocket Option password?

No. You log in to Pocket Option yourself, and Avalisa works on the Pocket Option page already open in your own browser, logged in as you. It does not move accounts or funds. Only ever type your Pocket Option password on Pocket Option's own site.

What changes from Demo to Pro?

Pro unlocks unlimited trades and both Martingale mode and Avalisa Bot mode (the rule-based signal mode, which is Pro-only).

Do I need a card for Demo?

No. Signing up and the 10 free Demo trades ask for no payment details; a card (or Apple/Google Pay) is only asked at the paid-plan checkout on the pricing page.

How do I pay for a paid plan?

Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, through the checkout on the pricing page.

If I buy Basic first, do I get credit toward Pro?

No. There is no credit or discount; Pro is always its full price ($119 one-time or $29/month).

How do I cancel the $29/month Pro subscription, and what happens after?

Email avalisapobot@gmail.com and we'll cancel it for you. After the subscription is cancelled or ends, your licence goes back to the free Demo plan (10 trades). Your account, settings and trade history are kept, and subscribing again upgrades it again.

Do I need to deposit money to use Avalisa PO Bot?

No — you don't need to deposit money to use Avalisa PO Bot. The free Demo plan includes 10 Martingale trades, webapp bot access, dashboard access, and basic trade history. Signing up and using those 10 free Demo trades asks for no card or payment details. We recommend starting on a DEMO account. A real Pocket Option account is optional — the bot works on both.
