What to ask about any “free bot”

Before you install anything advertised as free, ask three questions: what can the free version actually do, what stops after the free part, and is the paid part a one-time fee or a recurring subscription? A product that will not answer those plainly is not worth your trading account.

Avalisa PO Bot is a Google Chrome extension that places trades on Pocket Option in your own browser, on your own account. It is not a signal group and not a separate trading platform.

What the free Demo plan includes

  • 10 Martingale trades — enough to watch the bot enter, scale the stake after a loss and record the results.
  • Webapp bot access and dashboard access.
  • All supported timeframes.
  • Basic trade history — what the bot did and when.

Avalisa Bot mode (the rule-based signal mode) is a Pro feature and is not in the free plan. Run the free trades on your Pocket Option demo account so no real money is at risk while you learn how it behaves.

What costs money

Basic — $69, one-time. Paid once; it never renews and is never billed again. Unlimited Martingale trades, cloud settings sync and trade history.

Pro — you choose at checkout: $119 one-time (never renews), or $29/month, which is a real recurring subscription that renews every month until you cancel it. Both Pro options unlock unlimited trades, Martingale mode and Avalisa Bot mode.

Put simply: Basic is always one-time; Pro is $119 one-time or $29/month (a real recurring subscription). Payment is by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through the checkout on /pricing.

What a paid plan does not do: predict the market, guarantee results or change the risk of binary options. It removes the trade limit and, on Pro, adds a mode.

Why we say start on demo anyway

Binary options are high risk. At a 92% payout a win earns less than a loss costs, so you need to win more than 52.1% of trades just to break even (break-even = 1 / (1 + payout)). No bot, this one included, guarantees profit or any win rate.

Martingale multiplies the stake after each loss, and a losing streak can wipe out a balance. In the panel, Martingale Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle — at 2x, Steps 3 means a full losing cycle costs 15 times the base stake. Cap Steps, and watch a losing streak on demo before anything else. See Martingale risk explained.

How to get started

  1. Install Avalisa PO Bot from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Open Pocket Option on its demo account.
  3. Follow the setup guide.
  4. Run the free Martingale trades with a capped Steps value and read the history, including the losses.
  5. Only if you want more than the free trades, compare plans on /pricing.

Keep the Pocket Option tab visible while the bot trades: a background or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection.

Risk statement

Trading binary options is high risk. The bot does not guarantee profit. Test on a demo account first, and only trade with money you can afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

Is the free plan a real bot or just a sign-up funnel?

It is a working plan: 10 Martingale trades, webapp bot and dashboard access, all supported timeframes and basic trade history. Its limit is the trade count, and Avalisa Bot mode is Pro only.

Do I have to pay every month?

No. Basic is $69 paid once, and Pro can be $119 paid once. Only the $29/month Pro option is a recurring subscription, and you choose it yourself at checkout.

What is the difference between Martingale mode and Avalisa Bot mode?

Martingale mode multiplies the stake after a loss, up to the Steps cap you set; it is in every plan. Avalisa Bot mode is a rule-based signal mode where Intensity sets how many of its four rules must agree (Low 2, Mid 3, High 4); it needs Pro.

Can the bot promise wins?

No. Results vary with the market, payout, pair and settings. Judge it on demo yourself.
