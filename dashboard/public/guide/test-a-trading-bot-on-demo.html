Why a demo account first, not later

A Pocket Option demo account uses virtual money, so every outcome costs nothing. That sounds trivial, but it is the only cheap way to answer questions you cannot answer by reading documentation:

  • How does the stake plan behave in a real losing streak, and how far can a streak run before it exhausts your balance?
  • Does the bot keep working the way you expect while your tab is visible?
  • Do the pair, timeframe and payout settings you chose actually trade at all, or do filters like a minimum payout % block most signals?
  • Do you understand the results well enough to judge a session on its own merits?

There is no shortcut that makes these answers free except doing the trading itself on virtual money.

Step 1: Install and connect with a demo account

  1. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store.
  2. Log in to Pocket Option with your demo account and keep that tab open. Keep the Pocket Option tab visible while the bot trades — a backgrounded or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection.
  3. Follow the setup guide at avalisabot.vercel.app/guide to link your Pocket Option account to the bot.

Step 2: Choose conservative settings for the test

The Martingale strategy doubles (or multiplies) the stake after each loss. That means a losing streak of several trades can erase a large share of your balance quickly — which is exactly what you want to watch happen to virtual money, not real money. For a test session:

  • Cap the Martingale Steps. Steps can be set to a number or to Infinite. There is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss-limit setting; limiting the number of steps is the real way to cap how far a losing streak can run. Pick a small number for testing. If the next stake would exceed your balance, the bot pauses — watch that happen deliberately. See the ready-made demo setups.
  • Start with the smallest multiplier (2x) rather than 2.2x–3x.
  • Set a Minimum payout % so trades only fire when the payout is high enough to matter.
  • Pick one timeframe you actually intend to use, and one pair-scanning mode (current pair only, auto scan favorites, or auto-switch favorite). Changing several things at once makes every result meaningless.

Step 3: Run the session and read what actually happens

Let the bot trade through complete win and loss sequences. While it runs, keep the Pocket Option tab visible and check:

  • Stake escalation. After each loss, confirm the next stake was multiplied exactly as configured, and that it stopped at your step cap.
  • Payout behaviour. Were some pairs filtered out by your minimum payout %? That is expected and normal.
  • Result detection. Did every trade register its outcome promptly? Delayed or missed detection usually means the tab was backgrounded.
  • History. Open the trade history afterwards. Read a full session end to end — including the losing half. A demo session you would be embarrassed to show someone else is a session you should not replay with real money yet.

Step 4: Decide what the test actually told you

A demo test answers process questions, not fortune questions. It can tell you whether the bot executes its plan correctly, whether your caps stop streaks where you want them, and whether your settings even produce trades. It cannot tell you whether the next week will be green or red — nobody's settings can. If the process behaved correctly and you understand every trade in the history, you are ready to decide for yourself what a real-money session's starting size should be, with money you can afford to lose.

Common mistakes

  • Testing with Infinite Martingale Steps. Watch the streak cap do its job before you consider removing it.
  • Backgrounding the browser tab and then concluding the bot “missed trades.”
  • Changing settings mid-session, so no session can be attributed to one configuration.
  • Jumping to real money after one good session. One green demo session measures nothing about the next one.

Risk statement — read before anything else

Frequently asked questions

Does anything here — demo account or paid plan — promise that trading will pay out?

No. The bot does not guarantee any result. A demo account costs nothing and shows how the bot behaves; it tells you nothing about future results.

Do I need a paid plan to test on a demo account?

No. The free Demo plan includes 10 Martingale trades on a demo account, webapp bot access, the dashboard, all supported timeframes and basic trade history.

The Martingale doubled my stake until the balance ran out. Is the bot broken?

No — that is the strategy doing exactly what it is designed to do, uncapped. Set a finite Martingale Steps limit and watch the cap engage. That is what the limit is for.

Can I use the bot on a real account straight away?

Yes, it works on real accounts. The advice, ours and the platform's implicit one, is to know the behaviour from demo sessions first.

Pocket Option's own blog says users can utilise automated strategies as long as they comply with platform rules; it does not name third-party extensions, and this is not their formal Terms of Service. Treat it as a signal to keep things tidy, not as a legal answer.
