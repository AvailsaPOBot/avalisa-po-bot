From Demo to a Real Account

The extension places trades the same way on a demo account as on a real one, so moving to a real account changes the money and the pressure, not the mechanics. Here is what stays the same, what changes, and the checklist to run before you switch.

What stays exactly the same

  • Trade placement. The extension does not run separate trade-placement logic for demo and real. It reads whichever Pocket Option account is active and places trades the same way on either.
  • Your settings. Strategy, Direction, multiplier, Martingale Steps, timeframe, Minimum payout % and Pair Scan are the same controls on both account types — see the settings guide.
  • The tab rule. Keep the Pocket Option tab visible while the bot trades; a background or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection. More in why the trading tab must stay open.

What changes when you go live

  • Real money. Every losing cycle now costs actual funds, not virtual credit.
  • Emotions. Watching a real balance move changes how people behave. Decide before you start that you will not change settings in the middle of a losing cycle.
  • Payouts. Check the payout shown on the real account before you start, and make sure it still clears your Minimum payout % setting.

Why results on demo and real can feel different is covered in why bots “win” on demo and lose on real; this page is the practical checklist.

Checklist before you switch

  1. The same settings ran on demo. Move over a configuration you have actually run on demo — not a new idea tested for the first time with real money. See how to test a trading bot on demo, or browse the ready-made demo setups.
  2. You know the full losing-cycle cost and can afford it. Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle; at 2x, Steps 3 costs 15 times the base stake. There is no separate stop-loss — limiting Steps is the cap, and the bot pauses when the next stake would exceed the balance. Work out your own numbers in the Martingale calculator.
  3. A small base stake. Size it so that full-cycle cost is an amount you can lose without changing your plans.
  4. The tab stays visible for the whole session.

How to switch the active account

Use Pocket Option’s own account controls to make your real account the active one. The extension has no demo/real mode of its own: it follows whichever account Pocket Option shows as active. Before you press start, check that the balance on screen is the real-account balance you expect.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to reinstall or reconfigure the extension for a real account?

No. There is no separate demo or real configuration; the same extension and settings work on whichever account is active.

What if the real-account payout is lower than on demo?

A lower payout raises the win rate you need to break even: at payout p, break-even is 1 / (1 + p) — 52.1% at a 92% payout. Check the payout shown and your Minimum payout % setting before trading.

Can I withdraw after bot trading?

Withdrawals go through Pocket Option’s own normal flow, the same whether trades were placed by hand or by the extension.

Does the bot still work on my demo account after I open a real one?

Yes. The bot works on both Demo and Real Pocket Option accounts, so your demo account keeps working after you open a real one.

Do I have to type my Pocket Option password into the bot?

No. You log in to Pocket Option yourself, and the bot operates the page already open in your own browser, logged in as you. Never type the password anywhere but Pocket Option’s own site.

Can I run the bot on my phone once I go real?

Yes. There is no app to install — open avalisabot.vercel.app/webapp in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android) and follow the steps it shows for that phone.

If I buy Basic first, do I get credit toward Pro later?

No. Buying Basic first gives no credit or discount toward Pro later; Pro is always its full price ($119 one-time or $29/month).
