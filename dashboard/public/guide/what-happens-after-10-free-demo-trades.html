What the 10 free trades include

The free demo plan costs nothing and asks for no payment details — signing up and the 10 free demo trades never ask for a card. What it includes:

  • 10 Martingale trades
  • Webapp bot access
  • Dashboard access
  • All supported timeframes
  • Basic trade history

What happens at trade 10

Nothing is lost. The bot simply stops trading and shows you the paid plans. You can still open the dashboard, read the basic trade history from your demo session — including the losses — and decide what to do next. A card (or Apple Pay or Google Pay) is only asked if you choose a paid plan, at the checkout on /pricing.

Basic vs Pro

Basic is always one-time; Pro is $119 one-time or $29/month (a real recurring subscription) — you choose at checkout.

PlanPriceWhat it unlocks
Basic $69 one-time only — never a subscription, never renews, never billed again Unlimited Martingale, cloud settings sync, trade history
Pro $119 one-time (never renews) or $29/month (a real recurring subscription that auto-renews every month until cancelled) Unlimited trades, Martingale mode + Avalisa Bot mode

Payment is by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay via the checkout on /pricing.

The free-Pro route

You do not have to pay at all. Pro is also granted to users who register a new Pocket Option account through our link and then link that Pocket Option ID in their Avalisa account. It is only granted when Pocket Option's partner system confirms the registration came through our link. When granted, this Pro is permanent — it never expires and never converts to a paid subscription. The full flow, including where the link and the claim form live, is in Free Pro access when you open a new Pocket Option account through our link. If you already have a real account and are moving from demo, see Moving from a demo to a real account: the checklist.

Keep testing on demo

You do not need to decide at the cap. A demo account costs nothing and always lets you observe how the bot behaves — its stake plan and its reaction to losing streaks. It tells you nothing about future results, but it is the right place to learn the behaviour before any real money is involved. If you later fund a real account, follow the demo-to-real checklist first.

Risk statement

The bot does not guarantee profit. Martingale doubles the stake after each loss. Only use money you can afford to lose.
