What the offer actually is

Avalisa PO Bot is a Chrome extension that automates trades on Pocket Option inside your own browser. The Pro plan normally costs money — at checkout you choose either a $119 one-time payment or $29/month (a real recurring subscription that auto-renews until you cancel it). Both unlock unlimited trades, Martingale mode, and Avalisa Bot mode (the rule-based signal mode that is Pro-only).

When you create a brand-new Pocket Option account through our link instead, you can receive that same Pro access for free. The key conditions, stated plainly:

  • The Pocket Option account must be new and registered through our link.
  • Pocket Option's own partner system must confirm that the registration came through our link. We do not grant the plan on our side alone; the confirmation comes from Pocket Option.
  • You must then link that Pocket Option ID in your Avalisa account.

No condition here is about trading performance. Nothing about how well the bot trades affects whether the plan is granted — it is purely about how the account was registered.

For transparency: this is an affiliate arrangement. When someone registers a Pocket Option account through our link, we earn a commission from Pocket Option. That is what funds the free grant.

What you get once Pro is active

  • Unlimited trades on both modes available to Pro accounts.
  • Martingale mode: direction (Always Buy, Always Sell, or Alternating), a loss multiplier (2x, 2.2x, 2.4x, 2.6x, 2.8x or 3x), a step limit, trade timeframe, minimum payout %, and pair scan options.
  • Avalisa Bot mode: rule-based signals with an intensity setting (Low / Mid / High).
  • The extension works on both Demo and Real Pocket Option accounts, and everything works identically on the free grant and the paid plan.
  • The grant is permanent. It does not expire and does not convert into a paid or recurring subscription later — it is the same "never billed again" status as a one-time purchase.

Step by step

  1. Log into (or create) your Avalisa dashboard account, then register a new Pocket Option account through our link. The registration link is the "Open Pocket Option" button on the Avalisa site (it appears on the home and pricing pages, and on the sign-up page) — it is not on this guide article. Use a fresh Pocket Option account, not one you already own.
  2. Wait for Pocket Option's partner confirmation. The plan is only granted when Pocket Option's partner system confirms the registration came through our link. If that confirmation never happens — for example because a browser setting blocked the referral, or the account turned out to be an existing one — the plan will not be granted.
  3. Submit your new Pocket Option ID in the dashboard. The submission form is inside your logged-in Avalisa Dashboard, in the Settings tab, in the card headed "Link Your Pocket Option Account". It is shown while you are on the free plan with no Pocket Option ID linked yet — it is not on this public website's article pages. Enter the numeric Pocket Option UID and submit it. If Pocket Option's partner system has already confirmed your registration, Pro is applied immediately. If it has not, the claim is queued for a manual review before the plan activates. We know that review has sometimes taken longer than we would like — if it has been several days, email support (below) with your Pocket Option ID rather than assuming something is broken.
  4. Check your status. Anyone can check whether a given Pocket Option ID already has a plan on the guide page ("Check my Pocket Option ID"). If the ID shows no plan, that is your answer — contact support rather than assuming a delay.

If step 4 shows nothing, the usual causes are: the registration did not come through our link, the account was not new, or the Pocket Option ID linked in the Avalisa account does not match the registered account. Contact support at avalisapobot@gmail.com with your Pocket Option ID and your Avalisa account email so the backend can be checked. Do not assume activation until that check is done.

Read this before you trade: risk

Binary options trading is high risk. You can lose your entire balance, and you should only ever use money you can afford to lose.

  • The bot does not guarantee profit. No setting guarantees it. Our own measured data shows no setting beating break-even over large samples, and past performance never predicts future results.
  • Martingale is where balances go to die. It raises the stake after each loss — at a 2x multiplier five straight losses (Martingale Steps 4) cost 31× your base stake, and at 3x they cost 121×. A long losing streak can wipe the account. Limit the number of Martingale steps; there is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss-limit setting, and the bot pauses when the next stake would exceed your balance.
  • Start on a demo account. The Demo plan is free for everyone anyway, and the extension behaves the same way on demo and real accounts. Spend time understanding the settings, the payout floor, and the step limits before any real money is involved.
  • Keep the Pocket Option tab visible while the bot trades; a backgrounded or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection.
  • Pocket Option's own blog says users "can utilize automated strategies as long as they comply with platform rules," but that is not a statement from their formal terms of service, and we will not claim Pocket Option "approves" any third-party extension.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a brand-new Pocket Option account?

Yes. The offer is for users who register a new Pocket Option account through our link. An account you already have is not a new registration.

Why wasn't my plan granted?

The most common reason is that Pocket Option's partner system did not confirm the registration came through our link. Use the ID check on the guide page first; if it shows no plan, email support with your Pocket Option ID and your Avalisa account email.

Does free Pro cost anything later?

No. The grant is permanent — it does not expire and does not convert into the $29/month subscription or any other charge later.

Is free Pro different from paid Pro?

No features differ — both unlock unlimited trades, Martingale mode, and Avalisa Bot mode. They differ only in how you obtained them.

Can you tell me what results to expect?

No. We will not quote a number as a promise: outcomes vary by market, payout, pair, timeframe, and settings, and no setting we measured beat break-even over large samples. Test on demo and judge for yourself.
