What our measurements show

In our September 2026 measurements over large samples, no setting combination beat break-even. At a 92% payout, break-even is a 52.1% win rate (1/(1+p)); Martingale measured around 43–44%. Settings do not find a winning configuration — they decide how much you risk when you are wrong. Details: honest win rates and break-even.

Strategy and intensity

  • Martingale is available on every plan and trades in the direction pattern you choose.
  • Avalisa Bot (Pro) is a rule-based signal mode. Intensity sets how many of its four rules must agree: Low (2 of 4), Mid (3 of 4), High (all 4). More required rules means fewer trades. Start at High on demo so each decision is easy to follow.

Direction and pair scan

  • Direction (Martingale): Always Buy, Always Sell or Alternating. None of them beat break-even in our measurements, so pick one and keep it fixed while you test anything else — changing two settings at once tells you nothing.
  • Pair Scan: Current pair only, Auto scan favorites, or Auto-switch favorite. Scanning more pairs means more trades and less clarity. Start on Current pair only.

Multiplier and Martingale Steps — where the risk lives

Martingale raises the stake after each loss; a losing streak can wipe out a balance. Two settings bound it:

  • Multiplier after a loss: start at 2x. 2.2x to 3x make every later trade in a losing cycle more expensive.
  • Martingale Steps: Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle; at 2x, Steps 3 costs 15 times the base stake. There is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss-limit setting — limiting Steps is the cap, and the bot pauses when the next stake would exceed the balance. Start small, for example Steps 3.

Work out the exact cost of your multiplier and Steps in the Martingale calculator before you use them.

Timeframe and the payout filter

  • Timeframe (trade expiry): there is no measured “best” one. Pick one, hold it constant and judge it on your own demo history — see choosing a timeframe.
  • Minimum payout %: skips markets paying below your threshold. A higher payout lowers the break-even win rate (about 52.1% at 92%, 58.8% at 70%), but a filter only changes which markets you trade — it does not make a losing strategy win. Read why the payout filter matters.

A cautious starting point

  1. Demo account first. See how to test a bot on demo.
  2. Martingale at 2x with Steps 3, or Avalisa Bot at High; Current pair only.
  3. Keep the Pocket Option tab visible — a background or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection.
  4. Change one setting at a time and judge it on your own results.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a setting that wins?

No. We found no setting combination that beat break-even over large samples. Settings shape risk, not outcomes.

Why does Steps matter more than the multiplier?

Steps caps how many trades a losing cycle can contain; there is no separate stop-loss. The multiplier only changes how expensive each later trade is.

Can I go to a real account right away?

Only after the same configuration has run on demo and you know the cost of a full losing cycle.
