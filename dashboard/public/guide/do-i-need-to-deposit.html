The short answer

Using Avalisa PO Bot needs no deposit and no payment to start. The free Demo plan includes 10 Martingale trades, webapp bot access, dashboard access, all supported timeframes and basic trade history. Signing up and using those 10 Demo trades asks for no card or payment details.

The bot works on both Demo and Real Pocket Option accounts. We always recommend starting on a demo account, so a real-money Pocket Option balance is optional while you learn how the bot behaves.

Two different kinds of money

People often mix up two separate things. They are not connected:

  • Paying for an Avalisa plan. Only if you choose to, after the Demo. Basic is $69 one-time and never renews. Pro is your choice at checkout: $119 one-time, or $29/month, a real recurring subscription that renews every month until cancelled. You pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay at the checkout on /pricing — that is the only place a card is asked for.
  • Money in your Pocket Option account. That is between you and Pocket Option. You log in to Pocket Option yourself; the bot operates the Pocket Option page already open in your own browser and does not move accounts or funds. Avalisa never takes a deposit.

So you can use the free Demo plan on a Pocket Option demo account without spending anything at all.

The risk you should know first

  • The bot does not guarantee profit. No setting has been measured to beat break-even over large samples.
  • Martingale grows your stake after every loss. At a 2x multiplier, a 5-trade losing cycle costs 31× your base stake. Limiting Martingale Steps is the real way to cap a losing streak — there is no separate stop-loss setting.
  • The extension trades the same way on demo and real accounts, so what you see on demo is how it behaves — which is exactly why demo comes first.
  • Before adding money, work through the Real Account Go/No-Go Checklist and confirm every item passes.

Trading involves risk; never trade money you can't afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a card to try Avalisa PO Bot?

No. Signing up and the 10 free Demo trades ask for no payment details. A card, Apple Pay or Google Pay is only asked at the paid-plan checkout on /pricing.

Do I need a real Pocket Option account with money in it?

No. The bot works on Demo and Real Pocket Option accounts, and we recommend starting on a demo account.

Does Avalisa ever hold or move my money?

No. You log in to Pocket Option yourself and the bot operates the Pocket Option page open in your own browser. It does not move accounts or funds. Never type your Pocket Option password anywhere except Pocket Option's own site.

Is any paid plan a subscription?

Only one option. Basic ($69) and Pro $119 are one-time and never renew. Pro $29/month is a recurring subscription that auto-renews until cancelled. See pricing questions answered.
