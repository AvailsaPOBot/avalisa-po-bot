How the Steps setting maps to trades

In the Avalisa PO Bot panel, Martingale Steps counts recovery steps after the first trade: Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle, then the stake returns to the start amount. So at 2x, Steps 3 places $1, $2, $4, $8 on a $1 start — 15× the start amount if all four lose. A win at any point also returns the stake to the start amount.

The bot pauses when the next stake would exceed the available balance. If you enter a balance above, any trade the balance could not cover is marked in red.

How to use the number before you set Steps

  • Read the total as the price of one bad streak. Each extra step at 2x adds a stake as large as everything before it combined: Steps 3 to Steps 6 takes a $1 cycle from $15 to $127.
  • A streak longer than the cycle does not stop at the total. After a full losing cycle the stake returns to the start amount and a new cycle begins, so a longer run of losses keeps costing.
  • Higher multipliers cost more at every step. Compare the 2x and 2.2x tables at the same Steps.
  • Limiting Steps is the real cap. There is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss-limit setting; the balance pause is a last-resort brake, not a plan.
  • Watch a full losing cycle on demo first. The free demo includes 10 Martingale trades — enough to see one happen before any real money is involved.

Risk statement

Trading binary options is high risk; only trade money you can afford to lose. Martingale multiplies the stake after each loss and does not change the odds of any single trade — a losing streak can wipe out a balance. This calculator shows what is at risk, not what you will win. Trading involves risk. Avalisa does not guarantee profits. Start on a demo account.

Frequently asked questions

Does Steps 3 mean three trades?

No. Steps counts recovery steps after the first trade, so Steps 3 allows four trades in a losing cycle.

Which multipliers does Avalisa PO Bot offer?

2x, 2.2x, 2.4x, 2.6x, 2.8x and 3x, all in the calculator above.

What happens when my balance cannot cover the next stake?

The bot pauses instead of placing it. Choosing Steps so that a full cycle fits well inside your balance avoids reaching that point.

Can the calculator tell me which setting will be profitable?

No. It only adds up stakes. Our own large-sample measurements found no setting that beat break-even.
