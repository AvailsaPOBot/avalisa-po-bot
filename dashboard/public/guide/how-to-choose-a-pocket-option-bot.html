What you are actually buying

A Pocket Option bot is software that places trades on your Pocket Option account for you. It decides when to trade and how much to stake. It cannot change what a winning trade pays or whether the market moves your way. A seller who suggests otherwise has told you what you need to know.

1. Does it run in your own browser?

Prefer a bot that works on the Pocket Option site you already use, in your own browser. Avalisa PO Bot is a Google Chrome extension: it runs on the Pocket Option tab you are signed into, on your own account. If a product asks you to download an unknown desktop file or hand your account to their website, ask exactly why before paying. If you buy from a person rather than a store listing, see buying a bot from a seller.

2. What happens after a losing trade?

This question filters out the most dangerous products. A fixed-stake bot loses one stake at a time. A Martingale bot raises the next stake to win back the last loss, and the cost grows fast: six losing trades in a row cost 63 times the base stake at 2x, and 364 times at 3x.

So ask: how many recovery steps will it run, and what does a full losing cycle cost? In the Avalisa panel, Martingale Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle before it resets — Steps 5 at 2x is those six trades, 63 times the base stake. Limiting Steps is the real cap; there is no separate stop-loss or daily-loss setting, and the bot pauses when the next stake would exceed your balance. If a seller cannot answer this question plainly, walk away. More in Martingale risk explained.

3. Can you set a minimum payout?

Payout changes by pair and over time, and a lower payout means you must win more often just to break even: break-even win rate = 1 / (1 + payout), so 52.1% at a 92% payout and higher below that. Check that the bot has a minimum payout % setting so thin trades are skipped. Avalisa has one in the panel. See why the payout filter matters.

4. Is it honest about how it reads results?

A browser bot has to see each result to decide the next stake. Ask how it does that. Avalisa is upfront about its limit: keep the Pocket Option tab visible while it trades, because a background or minimised tab can delay trade-result detection.

5. Will it let you test on demo first?

Run any bot on a Pocket Option demo account before real money, with the timeframe, pairs and stake you actually plan to use, and watch how it handles a losing run. Avalisa works on Demo and Real accounts and its free Demo plan gives you 10 Martingale trades. A seller who pushes you to skip demo is giving you data. Our demo testing guide shows how.

6. What does the broker say?

Pocket Option's own blog says automated strategies are fine as long as they comply with platform rules, and names market manipulation — not automation — as the explicit ban risk. That is their blog, not their formal Terms of Service, which we have not read directly; for a definitive answer on your own account, ask Pocket Option support.

7. Is the price clear before you pay?

Five-star screenshots and self-awarded badges are cheap. Ask for things that are harder to fake: a public price list that says plainly what renews and what does not, and a support address that answers a real question. Ours is avalisapobot@gmail.com.

Risk statement

Trading binary options is high risk. No bot, Avalisa PO Bot included, guarantees profit or changes the odds — at a 92% payout you need to win more than 52.1% of trades just to break even (1 / (1 + payout)). Martingale multiplies the stake after each loss and a losing streak can wipe out a balance: in the panel, Steps = N allows N + 1 trades in a losing cycle, so at 2x, Steps 3 costs 15 times the base stake. Capping Steps is the real limit; there is no separate stop-loss setting. Start on demo and only trade money you can afford to lose.

Frequently asked questions

Can a bot tell me whether my trades will win?

No. The market decides each result. A bot chooses entries and stakes; it does not change what a trade pays.

Why push demo so hard?

Because it is the only test that costs nothing. Results differ by pair, payout, timeframe and settings, and demo results do not promise the same on a real account.

Does Avalisa take over my Pocket Option account?

No. It is a Chrome extension that works on the Pocket Option tab you are already signed into, in your own browser.
